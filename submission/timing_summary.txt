STARVATION-AWARE ROUND-ROBIN ARBITER
SKY130 HD PRE-LAYOUT TIMING ANALYSIS

Clock period: 10 ns
Target clock frequency: 100 MHz
Timing tool: OpenSTA 2.4.0

Process Corner    Max Path Delay    Worst Max Slack    Min Path Delay    Worst Min Slack
TT (Typical)      3.23 ns           5.77 ns            0.43 ns           0.46 ns
SS (Slow)         6.21 ns           2.79 ns            0.82 ns           0.94 ns
FF (Fast)         2.84 ns           6.16 ns            0.36 ns           0.39 ns

CONCLUSION:
The mapped arbiter was analyzed using the TT, SS, and FF SKY130 HD
Liberty timing libraries. All reported maximum-delay and minimum-delay
checks have positive slack under the specified timing constraints.
The SS corner exhibits the longest maximum-path delay of 6.21 ns.
These results are pre-layout estimates using an ideal clock network.
Post-layout timing analysis with extracted parasitics is required to
evaluate the final physical implementation.
